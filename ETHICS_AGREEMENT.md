# Lab Ethics Agreement

Cybersecurity skills can protect people or harm them. In this course you will learn attack techniques
so you can understand and defend against them. Sign this before Week 1.

I agree that:

1. I will only test systems, files, apps, and AI models that my instructor provides for this course.
2. I will never use course techniques against real accounts, school systems, networks, devices, or people
   without written permission from the owner.
3. I will not try to crack real passwords, read other people's data, or scan networks outside the lab.
4. I will use AI tools only as my school's policy and my instructor allow, and I will say when AI helped me.
5. If I find a real security problem (for example in a school system), I will report it to my instructor
   and not exploit it.
6. I understand that unauthorized access to computer systems is illegal in most countries and can
   lead to suspension, expulsion, or criminal charges.
